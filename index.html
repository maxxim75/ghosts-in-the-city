Survive Tokyo.

Meteorites shattered Tokyo. The city is now abandoned, hostile and filled with new threats. Four survivors — the “Ghosts” — must explore, track, fight and survive.

Before Ghosts in the City, Acid Pixel Studios shipped Transporter — a technical platform-racing game where speed, balance and vehicle choice are as important as reaching the finish line.
