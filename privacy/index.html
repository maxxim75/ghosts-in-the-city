Last updated: August 23, 2026

GITC Social Dashboard is operated by Acid Pixel Studios.

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application may access information authorized by that user through TikTok's API, including basic account information and statistics about published videos such as views, likes, comments and shares.

This information is used only to provide social media analytics and performance reporting.

We do not sell personal data or use TikTok data for advertising.

Access credentials and authorization tokens are stored securely and are not exposed publicly.

Data may be retained for analytics purposes while the account remains connected. Access can be revoked by disconnecting the application or removing its authorization from TikTok.

Third-party services, including TikTok, are governed by their own privacy policies.
